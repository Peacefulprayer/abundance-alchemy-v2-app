Collection

What Information We Collect

Abundance Alchemy collects only the information needed to create accounts, support guided practice features, protect the service, and operate optional user tools.

The app may collect the following categories of information:

The service is built around accounts, saved progress, optional personal content, and private media uploads.

Account information

Name, email address, encrypted password, and related login/session information.

Practice profile data

Focus areas, streaks, levels, completed sessions, reminder preferences, and related progress data.

User-created content

Custom affirmations, custom prayers, reflection text, and similar content users choose to save.

Uploaded audio

Audio files users upload for personal use within the app, plus basic file metadata needed to serve that content.

Security and technical data

IP address, session identifiers, reset-token records, device/browser user-agent strings, and authentication event logs.

Optional AI prompt context

If a user uses AI-generated copy features, the app may send a limited prompt including feature type and focus-area context to an AI service provider.

Use

How We Use Information

Abundance Alchemy may use information to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Authenticate users and protect account access
  • Save progress, preferences, and user-created practice content
  • Deliver optional private audio and companion features
  • Send password-reset or account-related emails
  • Detect misuse, fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Support optional AI-generated copy features requested by the user
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service

No sale of personal information

Abundance Alchemy does not sell personal information. If that changes in the future, this policy should be updated before the change takes effect.

Sharing

When Information May Be Shared

Service providers

Information may be processed by hosting, email, infrastructure, or security providers that help run the app.

AI provider

If a user chooses an AI-copy feature, limited prompt data may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate the requested result.

Legal or safety reasons

Information may be disclosed if reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce rights, or protect users, the service, or the public.

Retention

How Long Information Is Kept

Account, progress, and saved-content data may be kept for as long as needed to operate the user account and provide requested features.

Security logs, password-reset records, and related protection records may be retained for a reasonable period needed to prevent fraud, investigate misuse, and maintain service security.

User-uploaded audio may be deleted when the user deletes it inside the app, when the associated account is deleted, or when the service no longer needs to retain it.

Retention should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the service, security, legal obligations, and user-requested functionality.

Choices

User Choices, Deletion, and Requests

Users can choose whether to create an account, whether to save content, whether to upload private audio, and whether to use optional AI features.

Users may delete some saved content directly inside the app where those controls are provided. For account-deletion or privacy-related requests, users should use the developer contact method listed on the app website or applicable app-store listing.

If a user believes account information is inaccurate, they may also request correction or update through the same contact path.

Security

Abundance Alchemy uses password hashing, session controls, CSRF protection, rate limiting, and other administrative and technical measures designed to reduce misuse and unauthorized access.

Updates

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the service, legal obligations, security practices, or data handling. When updates are made, the effective date on this page should also be updated.
